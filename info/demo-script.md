## Scene-by-Scene Script

**Duration**: 30 seconds  
**Visual**: Black screen → NPREWS logo fade in

**Narration**:
> "Last year, a major sovereign fund lost $2.3 billion when a policy shift they didn't see coming forced regulatory intervention in their core holdings.
>
> The data was there. The signals existed. They just weren't connected.
>
> This is NPREWS — the system that connects the dots before they become headlines."

>

> **Cluster Agent** — Where does this fit? This narrative clusters with 'protectionist' policy signals we've been tracking for months.

## Demo Checklist

### Before Recording

- [ ] Seed database with demo data (`npx convex run seed:seedAll`)
- [ ] Clear browser cache for clean UI
- [ ] Set browser zoom to 100%
- [ ] Close unnecessary tabs
- [ ] Disable notifications
- [ ] Test microphone levels

### Key Points to Emphasize

1. **Human-in-the-loop** — AI assists, humans decide
2. **No pre-filtering** — Users see all signals
3. **Audit trail** — Every decision traceable
4. **Portfolio-specific** — Not generic news, YOUR exposure
5. **Regime bands, not predictions** — Position, not probability

### Potential Questions & Answers

**Q: "How is this different from Bloomberg Terminal?"**
> "Bloomberg tracks markets. We track the narratives that move markets — 6-18 months before they show up in prices."

**Q: "Can the AI be wrong?"**
> "Absolutely. That's why everything is auditable and human-validated. We show you the reasoning, not just the conclusion."

**Q: "What about false positives?"**
> "We surface signals. You decide relevance. Over time, the system learns your portfolio's sensitivity profile."

**Q: "Is this real-time?"**
> "Signals update continuously. Analysis runs on-demand or scheduled. Briefs require human review before distribution."
